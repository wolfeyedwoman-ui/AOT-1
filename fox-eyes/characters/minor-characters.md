# Minor Characters

## Wallis

Tenant farmer from out past the west fork, half a day toward Wall Rose. Broad-shouldered, decent, grateful, and mortified in roughly equal measure. His wagon wheel broke on the worst stretch of the west road in a lightning storm; Súile's detail brought him and his daughter to headquarters to wait it out. Produced two distinct mortally-wounded sounds during his daughter's question period. His wagon is promised mended by noon — the Captain's word on it.

## Mina

Wallis's daughter, six years old, a child artillery battery with total confidence in the importance of her news. Terrified of thunder until fed; devastating thereafter.

Rulings issued during her stay at headquarters:

1. The fox lady is the one with the yellow eyes. (*Correct.*)
2. "Is the little grumpy one your husband?" (*Under review.*)
3. Súile is bigger than the Captain — not the hips: the *hair* ("it goes *whoomf*"), the loudness, and the amount of room taken up. (*Methodologically sound.*)
4. The Captain is little and quiet like the cat that lives in her barn, which also doesn't like mud. (*Received as the year's finest compliment.*)

Her father says the Scouts are the brave ones who go outside the Walls and don't come back, and that you shouldn't stare when they ride past looking sad. The Captain's amendment: eat your stew, and let him worry about the sad ones.
