# The Special Operations Squad

Levi's hand-picked squad, per canon: chosen for skill and for the ability to coordinate without verbal communication. In 849 they live at the old headquarters, ride escort and recon rotations, and scrub flagstones in kerchiefs on their captain's orders.

## Petra Ral

Kind, warm, unfailingly professional — and devoted to the Captain in a way that is canon (her letters home worried her father toward the subject of marriage) and, in this story, quietly at war with itself over the new sergeant.

Petra's jealousy is Petra-shaped: it never once breaks her manners. It arrives as honey brought to an office that already has tea; as a pressed spare cravat mentioned in passing; as a smile that loses fifteen degrees rotating from captain to sergeant; as a teacup count, performed twice, in case the number improved. Her hostility ceiling is a sweetened remark addressed to a stew pot.

She is also genuinely excellent: first to the hearth with a scared child, first to volunteer the ration bookkeeping, first upright — ladle raised like a sabre — when a six-year-old questions whether the Captain is really a soldier. ("The Captain is *humanity's strongest soldier*. He's worth an entire brigade—")

Notably: she once measured Súile's height. "Last time" implies a standings table exists.

## Eld Jinn

Second-in-command. Sensible. Logs civilians in the night book, elbows Oluo at the correct moments, hears everything from the mess hall and repeats none of it.

## Gunther Schultz

Stern, reasonable, does not even pretend not to eavesdrop. Assigned with Oluo to fetch Wallis's wagon at first light.

## Oluo Bozado

Imitates the Captain's posture, speech, and disapproval, with a success rate best described as aspirational. Opined that *he* would never track mud past the threshold, then bit his tongue. Under standing orders not to lecture the farmer about titans. Will absolutely lecture the farmer about titans.
