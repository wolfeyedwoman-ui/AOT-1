# Fox Eyes

*An Attack on Titan story — the old Scout Regiment headquarters, year 849, before Eren Yeager.*

Captain Levi Ackerman commands his hand-picked Special Operations Squad from a half-forgotten castle inside Wall Rose. The regiment's newest transfer is Sergeant Súile Sionnach — "Fox Eyes" — a reconnaissance specialist the senior officers dismiss as "Pixis's girl." Levi doesn't dismiss her. He *catalogues* her, the way he catalogues everything: the mud she tracks in, the insolence she calls a salute, the recon work sharp enough to justify all of it.

A muddy evening, a stranded farmer, and a set of wagon ruts leading off the survey road pull the two of them into something the Military Police would very much prefer stayed in a timber barn off the west fork — and pull their public sparring somewhere quieter, up a spiral stair in the west tower, where the contraband books live.

## How this story is written

Turn-based role-play, kept as close to canon as the premise allows. Levi, the squad, the setting, and the political texture (Scout reputation, Military Police corruption, banned pre-Wall books) follow the source material; Súile and the smuggling plot are original. The Levi/Súile relationship is an established one — sharp-edged banter in public, something warmer in private — written with intimacy implied rather than depicted.
