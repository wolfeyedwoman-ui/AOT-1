# The Contraband Library

## The room

A forgotten room off the **west tower**, up a spiral stair, at the end of a corridor that goes nowhere else — library study and apothecary in the castle's more prosperous days. Súile chose it, officially, for the view. The shelves are lined with rotting books on **pre-Wall mythology and folklore**; the air smells of damp herbs. The hearth works. The door is never bolted.

## The collection

- **Sixty-odd volumes** by Levi's count, every one a burnable offense in the interior — "and they'd hang whoever shelved them from the same fire's smoke."
- The gaps between books are **fingered smooth**: she has read every single one, "before anyone sets them burning," and remembers the tales. Nobody knew until Levi read it off the shelves the way he reads everything.
- One volume of woodcut plates shows a wall-less horizon and water drawn in long impossible lines to the edge of the page, captioned in old script: ***the sea***.
- Nine of the volumes contain fox tales (see [fox-folklore.md](fox-folklore.md)).

## The danger

Books about the pre-Wall world are illegal by royal edict; possession is punished and collections are burned. (Canon grounding: Armin's grandfather's hidden book of the outside world.) The room's official story — "it came with the quarters" — survives casual inspection. It did not survive Levi's. His response was to pour the tea, dose hers with honey, and become the second person alive who knows: "Pixis didn't send us a scout. He smuggled a librarian past Erwin, and Erwin — who owns four books he shouldn't — will never hear it from me."

## Standing inventory, non-book

- One honey dish, formerly Petra's, permanently transferred.
- One salve tin, formerly Levi's, likewise.
- One lavender-oatmeal soap supply, homemade.
- One pail, hauled up the spiral stair nightly, unspilled.
