# Voss Grimes

**Rank:** New transfer to the Scout Regiment — a *defected* Military Police officer, which happens roughly never
**File:** Spotless. Suspiciously so. ("Files don't come out of the Interior clean. They come out *cleaned*.")
**Height / build:** 167 cm, 60 kg — skinny but tough, all sharp angles and hard lines. Pronounced Adam's apple.
**Hair:** Sandy blond, with a cowlick that drops his bangs over his forehead
**Eyes:** Grey-blue
**Face:** Handsome, charming, and deeply lined by years of chain-smoking and heavy drinking

## Presence

Holds himself like a kingpin. Wears his uniform fresh-pressed and spotless; fingers his cuffs when he's weighing the worth of something — or someone.

The hair is an instrument: when he's being theatrical, he **flicks** the bangs back; when he's being intimidating, he **smooths** them back with his hand. Neither is a tic. Both are orchestrated for impact, and rationed accordingly.

## Character

A malignant narcissist. Quick to anger and to outright fits of rage. A raging alcoholic — loudly begrudging, crude, classist, sexist, and, beneath the charm, murderous. He preys on pretty women: manipulates them into fawning if he can, pursues them relentlessly if he can't.

## Story position (as of Chapter 6)

- Súile flagged him first, on the dawn ride: a defected MP with a spotless file, arriving at a keep that sits half a day from an active military-supply smuggling route.
- Levi's read: nobody walks out of the Military Police into the death branch without a conviction or an assignment, and Grimes's transfer was signed *before* the barn was found — making him not a response but a **precaution**. Someone wanted eyes inside the one regiment that rides the west routes irregularly and can't be bought by the crate.
- The plan: don't spook him. Rotation stands, mess hall stays friendly, second sealed letter to Erwin for a back-channel check of the file. If genuine, he'll behave like every real transfer. If working, he'll angle — the map room, the duty rosters, or the soldiers who ride the west detail.
- Predicted approach vector: Súile herself. "He'll look at 'Pixis's girl' and see the easiest door in the keep." She has orders to let him think it — take nothing to him, let him bring things to her.

## Threat notes

- If he's a plant, his principal holds manifests and patrol schedules: depot clerk, MP officer, or quartermaster chain.
- Levi's contingency for the charm turning physical is on the record and involves doorframes.
