# Setting

## Time

Late autumn, **year 849** — after the fall of Wall Maria (845), before Eren Yeager joins the Scouts (850). Erwin Smith has been the Scout Regiment's 13th commander since 845. The Scouts are still the public's least favorite branch: a tax-funded suicide club with no victories to show, mocked in the interior and pitied in the villages. Nobody stares when they ride past looking sad.

## Place

The **old Scout Regiment headquarters**: a former castle deep inside Wall Rose, a remodeled relic of more prosperous times, kept by Levi's Special Operations Squad to a standard of cleanliness the castle has not enjoyed since construction. (Canon note: in the source material the old HQ is reoccupied for Eren in 850; this story takes the liberty of quartering Levi's squad there a year early.)

Geography as established:

- **The west road** runs toward the wall villages; its worst stretch is half a day out, near the **west fork**. Wallis farms past the fork.
- A **disused logging track** leaves the west road two kilometers before the fork, kinks at a **creek crossing**, and ends at a **timber barn** that sits on the junction of three farm lanes — invisible from the survey road, a day's easy cart from the **depot at the fork**.
- A **ridge** overlooks the fork; from it, ruts can be aged and traffic counted without contact.
- Inside the keep: officers' quarters cluster on one side; **Súile's tower room** sits alone off the **west tower**, up a spiral stair, opposite side of the keep, at the end of a corridor that goes nowhere else.

## Texture

- The Garrison and the Scouts trade personnel and condescension; a transfer arriving with Commander Pixis's blessing gets called "Pixis's girl" by officers who outrank her and underestimate her.
- The Military Police enforce, among other things, the royal ban on books about the pre-Wall world. Enforcement is a courtyard fire. The Interior's appetite for burning is one of the story's loaded guns.
- Levi's squad eats together in the mess hall, eavesdrops as a coordinated unit, and knows better than to comment.
