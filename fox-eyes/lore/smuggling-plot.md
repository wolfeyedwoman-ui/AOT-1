# Plot Tracker: The Barn on the Logging Track

*Status as of Chapter 6: dawn recon ride underway.*

## What Súile found (Chapter 4 report)

- Fresh ruts of **heavy wagons riding low**, leaving the west road two kilometers before the fork, turning onto a logging track the survey maps list as disused.
- An old **timber barn** at the track's end: chinks of lantern light after dark, four men who **moved like soldiers and dressed like they weren't**, a **guard dog**.
- Crates coming off the wagons at night. She got close enough — alone, on foot, downwind, in the rain — to read one stencil: **military supply stamps**. The long crates' size and fittings were wrong for grain, right for **maneuvering gear**.
- Bearings marked, horses counted, faces memorized. Withdrawal without contact.
- Map drawn from memory at the office table: track, creek kink, barn axis against north, downwind approach, dog noted, hayloft sightlines fanned out — whoever holds the loft owns the track for four hundred meters.

## Levi's read

- He has seen this trade **from the other end**: in the Underground, black-market gear "with the stamps filed off" came down the stairs "through men who moved like soldiers and dressed like they weren't."
- The barn wasn't found, it was **chosen**: junction of three farm lanes and a creek crossing, no line of sight from the survey road, a day's easy cart from the depot at the fork. Whoever picked it **reads route maps and knows the patrol schedule** — which means manifests, which means access, which means **a uniform somewhere up the chain**.
- Therefore this does **not** go to the Military Police, "because there's a decent chance it *is* the Military Police." (Canon grounding: MPs illegally selling government-issue equipment is established in the source material — the Marlowe Freudenberg subplot.)

## Actions taken

- **Sealed letter to Commander Erwin** in Trost, wings pressed in wax, addressed to bypass adjutants, out with the morning courier. Erwin "collects exactly this kind of ugly."
- **No squad briefing.** Not even Petra, yet. No mess-hall talk.
- **Dawn recon, fourth bell:** Levi and Súile only, disguised as two officers exercising horses — Levi in an anonymous brown coat, Súile in her never-oiled plain cloak. Survey road to the fork, then the ridge, **no closer**. Objectives: age the ruts, count the traffic, confirm her bearings. Súile has the lead from the creek onward.

## Open questions

- Who holds the manifests — depot clerk, MP officer, quartermaster chain?
- Where does the gear go: Underground buyers, interior collectors, or something worse?
- Is the traffic on a schedule, and did the storm interrupt a delivery?
- Four men and a dog at the barn — garrison strength or skeleton crew?
